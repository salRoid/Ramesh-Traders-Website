# Hero slides — plan

Five slides, one per product line. Paste each block into **RT admin →
Utilities → Store → Content → Add hero slide**, then upload its image.

The hero rotates every 7s in `sort_order`, pauses on hover, and dots let a
visitor jump. Turn a slide **Off** to hold it back without deleting it — useful
for running a seasonal set of three and parking the rest.

**You asked for three or four; this plans five** (nets, hooks, rope, thread,
mauli). Five is a long rotation — a visitor rarely sees past the third. My
suggestion: keep **3 live at a time** and rotate seasonally — nets + hooks
through the monsoon, mauli + thread through the festive months, rope year-round.

**Field limits:** headline ≤ 60 characters so it doesn't wrap badly. Body 25–45
words. Up to 3 proof points. Image 3:4 portrait, 1200×1600, bottom third quiet
(a caption sits over it on a dark gradient).
